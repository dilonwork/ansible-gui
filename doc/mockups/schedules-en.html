Recurring runs of job templates. A schedule launches the pinned template + commit, exactly like a manual launch.

Rules: schedules never edit a template — they launch the pinned commit as-is. Missed runs (backend down) are marked missed, not silently skipped. A schedule can require approval, in which case the run waits in the approvals queue instead of executing.
